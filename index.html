M.Sc. thesis, Technical University of Denmark (ArcLoRaM research project)

Robust Clock Synchronization Hardware for LoRa based Mesh Network Protocol

A resynchronization protocol for LoRa sensor nodes working in harsh weather (Greenland). I built a testbed that measures how far different oscillators drift from an atomic clock, stress-tested it in an environmental chamber, and used the data to model how much energy each clock choice costs.

Read the thesis (PDF, 68 pages)

External crystal vs. internal RC oscillator133-fold improvement (ppm)
Linear compensation algorithmup to 100-fold improvement over uncompensated oscillators
Cost of compensation10 minute calibration window at the start of a measurement
PlatformESP32, ESP-IDF, three devices measured in parallel
Grade10 on the Danish 12-point scale
PublicationConference paper from the ArcLoRaM project, in progress

What I did

  • Designed and built a system that reads the drift of several oscillators against an atomic clock.
  • Ran stress tests in an environmental chamber.
  • Derived an analytical model, based on Allan variance, for energy use and for the best device and network parameters, then fitted it to measured data.
  • Developed the linear compensation algorithm.

What I found

  • A crystal's drift depends little on how fast the temperature changes, but the rate does affect the tolerances of the compensation hardware.
  • Despite a longer guard time (and so a longer wake time between deep sleeps), the internal oscillator with an external crystal uses less power overall than a specified temperature-compensated oscillator module.

Research project, Technical University of Denmark

Mixed-signal DSP system-on-chip

A RISC-V based SoC for low-power signal processing: processor, memories and ADC/DAC peripherals, taken through open-source synthesis to a standard-cell netlist.

Read the research report (PDF)

CorePicoRV32IM, black-boxed into Chisel
MemoryHarvard: 4 KiB instruction, 1 KiB data
Synthesized area (Sky130 HD, pre-layout)about 32,800 µm² (0.033 mm²)
Sequential shareabout 44%, 592 flip-flops
PicoRV32 share of cellsabout 93%
Not included in the areaall memories (5 KiB instruction and data) and the core register file

What I did

  • Integrated the processor, memory system, ADC/DAC peripherals and synthesis flow.
  • Chose PicoRV32 for its Cadence Virtuoso compatibility and hardware multiplier.
  • Implemented a custom memory arbiter with round-robin bus arbitration and memory-mapped 8-bit ADC/DAC peripherals.

Tooling and verification

  • Verified each module with ChiselTest.
  • Scripted the RISC-V GNU toolchain flow that turns assembly into memory-initialized Verilog.
  • Wrote a custom Verilog emitter to connect Chisel to standard synthesis, then synthesized with Yosys/ABC and mapped to Sky130 HD.

CO2 monitoring chamber for peatland

An ESP32-based measurement system for peatland, where carbon emissions are significant. Earlier estimates ranged from 1 to 11 Mt per year, a wide range that needed a closer look. Project website: neskortes.is.

PCB revisions I designed3, each improving stability and performance
FirmwareC++ with ESP-IDF
Measured emissionsabout 12 Mt CO2 per year, just above the earlier 1–11 Mt estimate

What I did

Mechanics and field work
